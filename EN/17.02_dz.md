### Task 1.6. Find synonyms

1. audience → **d) spectators** (самый близкий в контексте конференции / презентации)  
2. anticipate → **d) foresee** (или **c) expect** — оба подходят, но foresee точнее в значении «предвидеть вопросы рецензентов»)  
3. candidly → **b) frankly** / **d) honestly** (оба очень близко, чаще берут frankly)  
4. lively → **d) animated** (или **c) stimulating** — animated чуть точнее для дискуссий)

### Task 1.8. Translate into English

1. One significant benefit of attending a conference is the opportunity to receive feedback from experts in your field on an early version of your work.  
2. New ideas can serve as tremendous / great inspiration for your own research.  
3. It is important for young researchers to share experience and receive feedback from colleagues.  
4. In your presentation, you can talk about the preliminary results obtained from your most recent experiment.  
5. Another advantage of attending a conference can be learning about the very latest findings in your field before they are published in journals.  
6. Rehearsing and giving an oral or poster presentation will later help you feel more comfortable in front of an audience.  
7. By listening to and participating in lively discussions at conferences, you can gain new ideas and refine existing concepts.  
8. Your conference presentations can demonstrate to potential employers and grant-awarding bodies your active engagement with / involvement in research.

### Task 1.11. Questions

1. Presenting preliminary results is useful because you can get **feedback from experts** right away → this feedback helps anticipate reviewers’ comments when submitting the paper for publication.  

2. Networking is important for:  
   - job hunting  
   - asking for advice from experts in other fields  
   - inviting speakers to your university  
   - getting support from people at the same career stage  

3. The best way to learn about the very latest findings → **attend conferences** (many results are shown there as preliminary / unpublished work before journal publication).  

4. Soft skills to improve:  
   - **presentation skills** (speed, level of detail, clarity)  
   - **communication skills** (answering questions, casual chatting with people)  

5. Social opportunities:  
   - travel to a new city / country  
   - try new food  
   - see local sights  
   - attend conference dinners, parties, trips  
   - meet people with similar interests → make friends  

6. Yes, it is very possible — if your «academic hero» is giving a talk or just attending, you can approach them, talk directly, and maybe even start future collaboration.  

7. Lively discussions can:  
   - give **new ideas**  
   - help **refine existing concepts**  
   - sometimes even **change your mind** about important issues in the field  

8. It is good for CV because it shows that you are **actively engaged** in your field, communicate with other researchers, and (especially if you presented) take part in sharing knowledge → looks great for employers and grant committees.
